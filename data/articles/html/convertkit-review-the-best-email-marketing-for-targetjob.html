Pros and Cons – The Good, the Bad, and the Ugly

  • Transparent pricing: You only pay for the contacts you actually have, and the price per thousand is clearly listed.
  • Strong deliverability: Built‑in compliance tools and a reputation for high inbox placement keep your campaigns reaching readers.

Where It Falls Short

  • Design flexibility: Landing pages and email templates are functional but lack the deep customization offered by some larger ESPs.
  • Cost per subscriber: Compared with bulk‑email services that charge a flat fee for massive lists, ConvertKit can feel pricey once you break the 5,000‑subscriber threshold.
  • Multichannel limits: No native SMS, push notifications, or chat integrations; you must rely on Zapier or custom APIs for those channels.
  • Learning curve for power users: While basic automations are simple, mastering advanced branching logic takes time.
  • Integration ecosystem: Direct integrations are limited; many popular apps (e.g., CRMs, e‑commerce platforms) require a Zapier bridge.
